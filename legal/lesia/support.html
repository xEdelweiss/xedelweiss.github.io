01Common questions

Do I need an account or internet?

No. There's no sign-up, and the app works fully offline.

Where are my favorites stored?

Only on your device. They're included in your regular device backups, so they move to a new phone set up from a backup. We never receive your data, so we can't restore it.

How do I clear my reading history?

In Налаштування (Settings), tap Очистити історію читання (Clear reading history). Your favorites stay.

A poem link didn't open the app.

If you've just installed the app on iPhone, go back and tap the link again. It will open the poem in the app.

Where do the texts come from?

The poems are checked against the academic Complete Collection of Works of Lesia Ukrainka (vol. 5, 2021). If you spot a typo, email us the poem's title and the line.

02Reporting a problem

Please tell us what happened, your phone model and system version, and the app version from Налаштування → Про застосунок (Settings → About). A screenshot helps too.
