02Your data

Do I need an account?

No. Workylog has no sign-up or sign-in. Open the app and start logging.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. The App doesn't sync to a server and sends nothing over the network, so your workouts never leave your phone unless you export them.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings and choose export. The App creates a JSON file with your profile, exercises, workout history and body-weight history, then opens the share sheet so you can save it to Files, AirDrop it or send it wherever you like.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Workylog's data is included in your regular device backups. If you set up your new iPhone from an iCloud backup or a backup made on your computer, your workouts come along. It's a good idea to export a JSON copy before you switch, just in case.

I deleted the app. Can you recover my workouts?

Unfortunately not. We never receive your data, so there's nothing on our side to restore. Your workouts can come back only from a device backup made before the App was deleted, or from a JSON file you exported earlier.

How do I delete my data?
  • Single items. Delete individual entries right in the App.
  • Everything. Use Wipe data in Settings to permanently delete all exercises and history.
  • Profile. Clear the profile fields in Settings.
  • Uninstall. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

03Using the app

If I delete an exercise, do I lose its history?

No. Past workouts that used the exercise stay in your history. Only the exercise itself is removed from your list.

How do I switch between kilograms and pounds?

Units are in Settings, along with the theme and your profile.

Do I need to fill in my profile?

No, the profile is optional. Add your name, body weight and height if you want to track your body weight over time.

Does Workylog work offline?

Yes, completely. It never needs an internet connection, so you can log in a basement gym with no signal.

Can I suggest a feature?

Please do. Email us with what you'd like to see and how you'd use it. Every suggestion gets read.

04Reporting a problem

If something isn't working, an email with a few details helps us fix it much faster:

  • what you were doing and what happened instead;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About);
  • the Workylog version (shown on the App Store page or in Settings);
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

Before writing, it's worth making sure you have the latest version of Workylog from the App Store, since the issue may already be fixed.
